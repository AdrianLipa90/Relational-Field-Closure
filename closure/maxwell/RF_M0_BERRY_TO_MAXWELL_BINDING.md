# RF-M0 — Berry Connection to Maxwell Binding

**Status:** STRUCTURAL_GAUGE_MATCH / PHYSICAL_NORMALIZATION_AND_SOURCE_OPEN

From RF-01,

\[
\mathcal A=-i\langle\psi|d\psi\rangle,
\qquad
\mathcal F=d\mathcal A.
\]

Under a local ray representative change

\[
|\psi\rangle\mapsto e^{i\chi}|\psi\rangle,
\]

the connection transforms as

\[
\mathcal A\mapsto\mathcal A+d\chi,
\]

while \(\mathcal F\) is invariant. Therefore

\[
\boxed{d\mathcal F=d^2\mathcal A=0}
\]

is an exact differential identity.

This is the structural candidate for the homogeneous Maxwell sector. Physical promotion requires an admitted dimensional scaling

\[
A^{\rm EM}_\mu=\alpha_A\,\mathcal A_\mu,
\qquad
F^{\rm EM}_{\mu\nu}=\alpha_A\,\mathcal F_{\mu\nu},
\]

plus a sourced action/current equation and empirical unit normalization.

The temporal/spatial split inherited from IDT gives candidate components \(\mathcal F_{\tau i}\) and \(\mathcal F_{ij}\); identification with electric and magnetic components remains a downstream test, not an RF-M0 premise.
