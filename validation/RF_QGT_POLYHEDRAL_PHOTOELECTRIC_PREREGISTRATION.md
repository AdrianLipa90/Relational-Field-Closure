5. **Refinement:** polyhedral subdivision preserves total oriented Berry flux and converges in metric/holonomy observables.
6. **Spinorial closure:** \(2\pi\) changes the spinor sign while \(4\pi\) restores the representative.

10. **Maxwell identity:** for the derived connection, \(dF=0\) must hold identically before sourced dynamics are tested.
11. **Lambda0 dimension:** every admitted contribution to \(\Lambda_0\) has dimension \(L^{-2}\) in the chosen unit convention.
12. **Bianchi bookkeeping:** dynamic \(\Lambda_0\) exchange and total stress-energy conservation must close without an untyped external source.
