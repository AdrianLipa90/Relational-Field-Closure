Z_RGB = diag(1, omega, omega^2)

and use the existing cyclic orbital shift

X_orb = [[0,0,1],[1,0,0],[0,1,0]].

Then X_orb^3 = Z_RGB^3 = I and

Z_RGB X_orb = omega X_orb Z_RGB.

Therefore the phase character and orbital shift do not commute. The scalar RGB roots alone remain Abelian.

Define W_ab = X_orb^a Z_RGB^b for a,b in {0,1,2}. The nine W_ab are Hilbert-Schmidt orthogonal and span M_3(C). Removing the identity and taking traceless Hermitian combinations gives a real dimension-eight space equal to Herm_0(3). Multiplication by i gives su(3). The standard Gell-Mann basis spans the same space.

Thus the exact representation chain is:

RGB character + ordered C3 orbital shift
-> finite Weyl pair
-> M_3(C)
-> traceless Hermitian dimension 8
-> su(3).

The non-Abelianity is generated by the relation between phase and ordered orbital transport, not by the three scalar phase labels alone.
