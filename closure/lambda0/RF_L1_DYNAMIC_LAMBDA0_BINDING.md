# RF-L1 — Dynamic Lambda0 Binding Contract

**Status:** SOURCE_CROSSREF_ADMITTED / DERIVATION_OPEN

The RFC Einstein target retains the dynamic scalar closure

\[
\boxed{
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda_0(x)g_{\mu\nu}
=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}T^{\rm total}_{\mu\nu}
}
\]

with \(\Lambda_0\) reconstructed from admitted scalar invariants rather than inserted as an arbitrary function.

Candidate inputs inherited from the pinned source program include scalar combinations of:

- informational/time fields,
- matter-field kinetic and potential invariants,
- electromagnetic invariant \(F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}\),
- additional scalar sectors that survive covariance and dimensional audits.

The older source ansatz is treated as a cross-reference target. RFC re-derives the allowed scalar basis and requires

\[
[\Lambda_0]=L^{-2}.
\]

Applying the Bianchi identity to the target form gives the transfer constraint

\[
\nabla_\mu T_{\rm total}^{\mu\nu}
=\frac{c^4}{8\pi G}\nabla^\nu\Lambda_0
\]

for the displayed convention. A full action-level derivation may redistribute this exchange into explicit \(\Lambda_0\)-sector stress-energy; the bookkeeping convention must be fixed before promotion.

The stationary limit must recover

\[
\nabla_\nu\Lambda_0=0
\]

and ordinary Einstein closure with constant cosmological term.
