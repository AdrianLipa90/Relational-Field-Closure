# RF-00 — Research and admission contract

**Status:** PASS_BOOTSTRAP_CONTRACT

## Goal

Test whether one common relational-phase-time construction forces the structures later recognized as Maxwell electrodynamics, Newtonian gravity and Einstein gravity, with a dynamic scalar \(\Lambda_0\) entering the Einstein closure.

## Forbidden shortcut

A target equation may be used as a validation oracle but not as an input to the derivation that claims to recover it.

## Admission levels

- `CANDIDATE`: mathematically typed proposal.
- `LOCAL_IDENTITY_PASS`: exact local identity proven/tested.
- `STRUCTURAL_PASS`: required symmetry/covariance structure recovered.
- `LIMIT_PASS`: target physical limiting law recovered without inserting it.
- `DERIVED_CANDIDATE`: all formal gates pass; empirical constants/data may remain.
- `EMPIRICAL_PASS`: preregistered external-data test passes.

## Mandatory audits

1. dependency provenance,
2. units/dimensions,
3. coordinate covariance,
4. gauge covariance where applicable,
5. conservation/Bianchi compatibility,
6. Newtonian weak-field limit,
7. Maxwell source-free and sourced limits,
8. no circular use of target equations.
