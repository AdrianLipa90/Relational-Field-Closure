\[

\[

\[

\[
\boxed{J_\mu J^\mu=-q^2+j_\mu j^\mu.}

## 3. Material velocity generated by the current

On that sector define

\[
\boxed{V^\mu:=\frac{j^\mu}{q}.}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{n_\mu V^\mu=0,}
\qquad
\boxed{\beta_{\rm phys}^2:=V_\mu V^\mu=\frac{j_\mu j^\mu}{q^2}<1.}
\]

Define

\[

\qquad
n_0:=\sqrt{-J_\mu J^\mu}.
\]

Since

\[
n_0=q\sqrt{1-\beta_{\rm phys}^2}=\frac{q}{\gamma},
\]

\[
\boxed{

Thus a future-timelike nonzero Noether current defines a unique future unit material four-velocity on its support.
